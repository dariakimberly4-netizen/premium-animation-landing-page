THE STARBUCKS STORY

From a narrow storefront to a place built around connection.

The story begins in Seattle’s historic Pike Place Market, where a single store offered fresh-roasted whole bean coffees. The company’s name drew inspiration from Moby Dick and the seafaring tradition of early coffee traders.

1971
CHAPTER 01 · PIKE PLACE

The beginning.

A single store in Seattle’s historic Pike Place Market focused on fresh-roasted whole bean coffee and the traditions surrounding it.

CHAPTER 02 · 1981

A first cup changes the path.

Howard Schultz first walked into a Starbucks store in 1981, was drawn in by his first cup of Sumatra, and joined the company a year later.

CHAPTER 03 · 1983

The coffeehouse idea.

A trip to Italy inspired a vision of coffee bars as places for conversation and community — a third place between work and home.

CHAPTER 04 · 1987

A new chapter begins.

After a period away building Il Giornale coffeehouses, Schultz returned in August 1987 and purchased Starbucks with local investors.

STARBUCKS IN THE PHILIPPINES

A new market, a natural cultural fit.

On December 4, 1997, the first Starbucks store in the Philippines opened at the 6750 Ayala Building in Makati City. The Philippines became Starbucks’ third market outside North America.

MORE THAN JUST COFFEE

Connection is the point.

The mission presented by Starbucks Philippines centers on nurturing the possibilities of human connection through every cup, conversation, and community — while bringing its coffee heritage and experience to life.

HOW THE MISSION LIVES

Six parts of the experience.

01 · OUR COFFEEC

Quality with care.

The page emphasizes quality, ethical sourcing, careful roasting, and improving the lives of the people who grow coffee.

02 · OUR PARTNERSP

More than a job.

Partners are described as people united by passion, diversity, respect, dignity, and the freedom to be themselves.

03 · OUR CUSTOMERSC

The cup connects people.

The customer relationship is presented as part of the same commitment to quality and the ongoing work behind every coffee experience.

04 · OUR STORESS

A place to belong.

Stores are framed as havens from the outside world — places for friends, belonging, enjoyment, and human connection at different rhythms of life.

05 · OUR NEIGHBORHOODN

Every store belongs somewhere.

The page positions each store as part of a community, with a responsibility to be a good neighbor and contribute through positive action.

06 · OUR SHAREHOLDERSS

Accountability matters.

Starbucks connects long-term success with delivering well across coffee, partners, customers, stores, and communities.
