1971
PIKE PLACE MARKET

The door opens.

In Seattle’s historic Pike Place Market, a single store offered fresh-roasted whole bean coffees. This is where the journey begins.

1981
A FIRST ENCOUNTER

One cup changes the path.

Howard Schultz first walked into a Starbucks store in 1981. His first cup of Sumatra left an impression; a year later, he joined the company.

1983
ITALY

The store becomes a coffeehouse.

A trip to Italy inspired a vision of coffee bars as places for conversation and community — a third place between work and home.

1987
A NEW CHAPTER

The idea comes home.

After building Il Giornale coffeehouses, Schultz returned in August 1987 and purchased Starbucks with local investors.

STARBUCKS IN THE PHILIPPINES

1997

On December 4, the first Starbucks store in the Philippines opened at the 6750 Ayala Building in Makati City. The Philippines became Starbucks’ third market outside North America.
